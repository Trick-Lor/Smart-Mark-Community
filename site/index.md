## Frequently asked questions

### Is Smart Mark free?

Yes. Smart Mark is completely free, with no ads and no accounts.

### Does it collect my data?

No. All marker layouts are stored locally on your device (chrome.storage.local) and are never transmitted anywhere.

### Which browser does it work on?

Google Chrome (Manifest V3). It runs on any normal web page, but not on chrome:// internal pages or the Web Store.

### Do I have to memorize shortcuts?

Every action has a keyboard shortcut, and all of them are rebindable from the options page. A toast shows the active mode as you work.
