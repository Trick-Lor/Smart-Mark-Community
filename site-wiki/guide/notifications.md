# Mode Toasts

A small floating pill confirms each mode change and shortcut press, so you always know what is active. You can turn it off or hide the key combo.

![Mode Toasts](/media/notifications.gif)

## Before you start

- Smart Mark installed.

## Step by step

1. Trigger any mode - a toast appears at the bottom of the page.
2. Press `Alt + T` to toggle the toast on or off.
3. Options > `Notifications`: switch `Show toast` and `Include shortcut` on or off, set `Toast duration` (0.5-5 s), `Toast fade`, and `Toast position` (`Bottom` or `Top`).

## Other notices

- `Desktop notifications`: a system notification when Smart Mark starts loading on a page and when it is ready (one per tab, not per frame). Off by default; switch it on in Options > `Notifications`.
- `Hide Layer toast` and `Hidden count badge`: the count shown after you hide a layer, and the number on the toolbar icon (see Hide Layer Mode).

## Tips

- Toggle combo display and Toggle desktop notifications have no default key. Give them one in Options > `Keyboard Shortcuts` if you switch them often.

## Shortcuts used here

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Alt + T` | toggle the shortcut toast on/off |

The combos above are the defaults; every one of them can be rebound on the Options page.
