# Undo and Redo

Step backward and forward through your marking actions, so a mistake is never more than one keypress away.

![Undo and Redo](/media/history.gif)

## Before you start

- Place or remove a few markers first.

## Step by step

1. Press `Ctrl + Z` to undo the last marker action.
2. Press `Ctrl + Y` to redo it.
3. Undo and redo cover creating and deleting markers, loading a slot, and hiding a layer.

## Tips

- With nothing to undo, `Ctrl + Z` goes to the page as usual (for example to undo your typing).
- `Undo depth` (10-500 steps) is under Options > `Marker Configuration`.

## Shortcuts used here

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl + Z` | undo the last marker action |
| `Ctrl + Y` | redo the last undone action |

The combos above are the defaults; every one of them can be rebound on the Options page.
