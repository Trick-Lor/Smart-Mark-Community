# Screenshot with Markers

Download a PNG of the page with your markers drawn on it - ready to paste into a bug report, a document, or a chat.

::: info
Available from Smart Mark 1.1.0. If your installed version is older, this feature arrives with the next Chrome Web Store update.
:::

![Screenshot with Markers](/media/screenshot-export.gif)

## Before you start

- Smart Mark installed.
- A page with at least one marker on it.

## Step by step

1. Mark what you want to point out.
2. Press `Ctrl + Shift + Alt + S`.
3. Smart Mark renders the page together with its markers and downloads `smart-mark-<site>-<date>.png`.
4. A toast confirms the download, or tells you when the page could not be rendered.

## Tips

- Toasts and the numbered image-marking boxes are left out of the picture.
- Layers you hid stay hidden in the picture (see Hide Layers).
- The image is made inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

## Shortcuts used here

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl + Shift + Alt + S` | download a PNG of the page with its markers |

The combos above are the defaults; every one of them can be rebound on the Options page.
